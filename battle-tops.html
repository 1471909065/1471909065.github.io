Visual building blocks
for your own game.

Colours are primarily stored as vertex colours. Fourteen small packed surface maps hold shared material constants. Complete world Prefabs include static ground collision setup; collision-only Prefabs are deliberately invisible.

Setup & integration

The current collection is prepared in Unity 6000.6.2f1. Materials use Unity glTFast 6.20.0; demonstration scenes use URP 17.6.0. Built-in and URP material rendering have been checked. Battle logic and physics behaviour are part of your project. Weather and FX pieces are static geometry.
