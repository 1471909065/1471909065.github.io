linxixi9527 creates stylized 3D asset collections for Unity projects. This portfolio focuses on the actual models, their included components and the ways they fit together.

The collections combine Codex-assisted initial shape and concept work with manual mesh cleanup, retopology, UV work and asset preparation. Detailed package information makes the included content and setup requirements clear.
